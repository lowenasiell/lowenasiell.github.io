↪ Work

Brief. The Swedish National Opera asked how opera and ballet stay relevant to a younger audience.

Approach. We built the concept "Stående Ovationer" (Standing Ovations) — a short film that borrows the emotional weight of a curtain call and hands it to everyday people, not performers, to make the feeling of opera relatable before anyone sets foot in the building.

Outcome. A campaign film and poster pitched to the Royal Swedish Opera as a way to reach a younger audience.
