05 / The logic behind the suggestion

Simple rules.
Transparent reasoning.

Snow conditions

Winter

Consider winter clothing, warm footwear or indoor activities.

Rain or precipitation

Rainy day

Consider rainwear, umbrellas or a message around home delivery.

22 °C or warmer

Summer

Consider sunglasses, cold drinks or other summer products.

8 °C or colder

Cold weather

Consider warm clothing, coffee or indoor activities.

Other conditions

Outdoor

Consider everyday products, fitness products or outdoor activities.

Rules are checked from top to bottom. Snow and precipitation take priority over temperature. These are example themes, not product-specific recommendations.

06 / Keeping it in perspective

A starting point.
Not the whole strategy.

Context is useful.

Local weather can inspire a more relevant message. A converted price makes the original SEK amount easier to understand in another currency. Together, they demonstrate how separate data sources can support one interface.

Judgement still matters.

The app does not account for product fit, margins, taxes, shipping, competitors or audience research. Reference exchange rates are not checkout quotes. Any campaign idea needs to be adapted and tested before launch.

Global possibilities.
A local perspective.
